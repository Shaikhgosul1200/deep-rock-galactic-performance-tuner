This isn’t about cheating the game out of its soul. It’s about **sculpting the experience** to match your playstyle. The suite provides granular, real-time adjustments to the game’s internal parameters, delivered through a **responsive, low-overhead UI** that respects your rig’s performance.

### Core Capabilities

- **Resource Refinement Engine** — Adjust the flow of Nitra, Gold, and crafting minerals. Fine-tune the economy to your liking, from “brisk” to “absurdly generous.”
- **Survivability Matrix** — Tweak shield recharge rates, health bars, and damage resistance with sliding scales. Perfect for players who want a *slightly* less lethal Hoxxes.
- **Mobility Vector Control** — Modify grapple cooldowns, jump height, and fall damage reduction. Fly across caverns like a drunken elf on a sugar rush.
- **Bosco & Team AI Dial** — Adjust the aggression and assist frequency of your robot companion and friendly AI dwarves. Make them proactive, not passive.
- **Horror Gauge (for the faint of heart)** — Reduce enemy spawn density or slow their speed. The glyphids won’t respect you, but at least they’ll be slower about it.

## 🧪 How It Works (High-Level)

The suite uses **memory-thread injection** to read and write specific game values. It does not modify the game files on disk, meaning your Steam library remains **pristine and verifiable**. This also means it’s **patch-resistant** — small game updates don’t break the core functions, as long as the global offsets remain stable.
